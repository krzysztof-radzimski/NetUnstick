Network diagnostics for Mac

Check your network path, DNS, internet connectivity and a chosen device without changing network settings.

The Mac App Store edition is read-only. It does not install a privileged helper or repair system routes, DHCP or VPN settings.

One purchase, no subscription or in-app purchases. Future updates to this app are included at no additional charge. NetUnstick adds no activation or device limit; use and redownloads are subject to Apple's App Store terms.

The App Store release is being prepared. It is not yet available for purchase.
