NetUnstick — Privacy policy

Local processing

NetUnstick has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or background uploads. Network observations are processed on this Mac. Device names, SSIDs, addresses, internal domains, credentials and packet contents are omitted or redacted from history and reports.

Network traffic

On your request, diagnostics resolve example.com using your resolver, connect to 1.1.1.1:443 and browse local Bonjour services. A device test contacts the endpoint you entered. DNS providers and contacted devices can see the usual network metadata, including the source address. No diagnostic report is sent to the developer.

Storage and deletion

Local history holds at most 20 sessions, 100 entries per session and 4 MB, including times, app/macOS versions, outcome and error codes and sanitized evidence. Delete it in Settings. Reports are saved only after a preview and your choice of file; delete exported copies separately. macOS manages the retention of its private system log entries.

Your choices

You can cancel checks and revoke Local Network access in System Settings. The full edition also lets you unregister its helper. Exporting or sharing a report is optional. Information you choose to send to a support provider is handled by that recipient.
