NetUnstick — Support

Run a check

Click Run diagnostics. Checks observe the connection; they do not change network settings. Open Network details to see each result. You can cancel an operation in progress.

Diagnostics and repairs

Read-only checks and individually confirmed repairs.

Get useful help

Reproduce the issue, run diagnostics and preview the relevant session in Activity. Share a redacted report with your administrator or contact support at kr@itorg.pl. Include what failed and when; omit private network details.

Narrow changes only

NetUnstick does not implement a VPN, change FortiClient/FortiGate policy, disable protections, flush all routes or reboot. Do not disable a firewall or managed security policy to obtain a green status. Ask your network administrator about managed VPN settings.

Contact

kr@itorg.pl

If you email support, ITORG processes the contact details and information you choose to send to answer your request. Send only the details needed; do not send passwords, credentials or unredacted network reports. You may ask about or request deletion of your support correspondence at the same address. Necessary records may be retained to handle the request and legal obligations.
