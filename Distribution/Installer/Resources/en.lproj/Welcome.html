Install NetUnstick

Diagnose connectivity and local device access problems after disconnecting a VPN.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later. Quit NetUnstick before continuing.

The installer places NetUnstick and its bundled helper in Applications. Administrator authorization is required to install.

Network repairs require separate confirmation and approval of the helper in macOS. Installation does not change network settings.
